### ▸ Option 1: M.Sc. Data Science Student Focus (Recommended)

```text
Excited to share a project I’ve been developing at the intersection of Data Science, Audio Signal Processing, and Machine Learning: SoundScope-AI — an orchestrated multimodal acoustic & lyric intelligence platform.

As an M.Sc. Data Science student working with acoustic datasets and NLP, I noticed how fragmented modern music analytics is:
▸ Audio DSP tools output raw numerical distributions (e.g., Spectral Centroid, RMS energy) without creative or contextual interpretation.
▸ Large Language Models provide generic music advice without grounding in physical audio data.
▸ Recommendation algorithms rely primarily on sparse metadata tags and historical listening logs.

SoundScope-AI explores how data-driven signal analysis and semantic modeling can be unified:

[01] High-Dimensional Feature Extraction (DSP)
Extracting acoustic fingerprints (20-band MFCCs, spectral moments, dynamic range, and harmonic-percussive separation) using Librosa and Essentia across benchmark datasets (Free Music Archive & GTZAN).

[02] Lyric & Poetic NLP Modeling
Automated transcription with Whisper, section-by-section emotional valence-arousal trajectory mapping, rhyme density quantification, and thematic clustering.

[03] Multi-Agent Orchestration (LangGraph)
Coordinating specialized domain agents (Signal Analyst, Lyric Semanticist, and a fine-tuned Mastering Critic LLM) into an explainable, structured production critique.

[04] Deterministic Verification Guardrails
A verification layer that cross-checks LLM critique statements against ground-truth audio physics to prevent model hallucinations.

[05] Interactive Analytical Dashboard
Real-time audio waveform visualization, acoustic radar charts, and synchronized multi-agent evaluation cards.

I would love to hear thoughts from data scientists, audio researchers, and music producers: What metrics or analytical perspectives do you find most meaningful when evaluating audio data?

Feedback and suggestions are welcome below.

#DataScience #MachineLearning #ArtificialIntelligence #AudioData #NLP #LangGraph #DeepLearning #Python #MultimodalAI #LLM
```
